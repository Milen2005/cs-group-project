Category: Financial Obligations Jurisdiction: United States

The lease agreement states a security deposit requirement of $3,500 on a monthly rent of $1,500. This exceeds typical statutory limitations in some regions.

💡 Recommendation:

State laws generally limit residential security deposits to 2 months' rent ($3,000 maximum). The current lease charges 2.3 months' rent. Request the landlord to amend the security deposit amount to comply with state statutes.

Category: Lease Term & Termination Jurisdiction: United States

Section 14 outlines an early lease termination notice requirement of 90 days along with a penalty fee of three months' rent.

💡 Recommendation:

A 90-day notice period is highly restrictive. Local standard tenant laws restrict early notice requirements to 60 days. Propose modifying the clause to request a 60-day notice period and cap termination penalties to a maximum of two months' rent.

Category: Repair & Habitability Jurisdiction: United States

Clause 9 states that the tenant is responsible for all repairs to major heating, cooling, plumbing, and electrical utilities under $250, and waives landlord liability for hot water outages.

⚠️ Legal Warning & Recommendation:

Landlords have an unwaivable duty under the statutory Warranty of Habitability to maintain heating, plumbing, and major utilities. Shifting repair burdens under $250 onto the tenant or waiving hot water liability is illegal. You should demand that the landlord remove this clause and restore full habitability coverage.
